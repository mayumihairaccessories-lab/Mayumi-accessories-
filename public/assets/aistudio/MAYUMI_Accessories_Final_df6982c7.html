01 — OUR STORY

Not just a hair clip.
A little piece of you.

MAYUMI is a Filipino handmade hair accessory brand that turns clay into small pieces of wearable art. Every design begins with an idea, shaped by hand and finished with care.

Inspired by creativity, culture, and contemporary femininity, we make accessories that invite you to express yourself in your own way—one colorful detail at a time.

How to order

  1. Ask about a design on our social pages or at the pop-up.
  2. Choose your clip and size, Gamay or Dako.
  3. Confirm your order details and payment with our team.
  4. We prepare and check your clip and pack it with care.
  5. Pick up at a meet-up, or arrange local delivery.
